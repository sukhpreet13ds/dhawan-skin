PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026

At Dhawan Skin and Laser Clinic, we are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our patients, clients, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and medical information when you interact with our website, book consultations, or utilize our clinic services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality dermatological, laser, and aesthetic healthcare services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Contact Information: Your full name, mobile telephone number, email address, age, gender, and residential location when you complete appointment bookings, request consultations, or submit inquiries through our website.
  • Consultation & Treatment Notes: Skin concerns, hair condition notes, treatment preferences, medical history summaries, or details shared voluntarily in consultation inquiry forms.
  • Appointment Scheduling Details: Preferred dates, consultation time slots, selected clinical services, and booking reference details.
  • Website Usage & Technical Data: Standard internet log data, including browser type, operating device, IP address, and page interaction timestamps to maintain platform security and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate medical, administrative, and clinical purposes, including:

  • Scheduling, managing, and confirming your in-clinic consultations and laser treatments.
  • Communicating clinical pre-procedure guidelines, post-treatment care instructions, and appointment reminders.
  • Responding promptly to inquiries sent via our contact forms, phone calls, or digital channels.
  • Maintaining confidential internal medical files in compliance with clinical record-keeping standards.
  • Improving clinic workflow, treatment accessibility, and patient care experiences.

3. Patient Confidentiality & Medical Privacy

Medical ethics and patient confidentiality are fundamental to our practice. Your consultations, treatment records, and personal health details are treated with the highest degree of medical confidentiality. Our clinical team and medical specialists, Dr. Navjot Kaur and Dr. Rohit Dhawan, adhere to rigorous medical standards to ensure your information remains strictly confidential.

4. Information Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or health data with third-party advertisers or commercial entities. Your information is only accessed by authorized clinic personnel involved in your care, or when strictly required by applicable legal authorities, court orders, or statutory healthcare reporting regulations.

5. Data Security & Retention

We maintain comprehensive administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure of your information. Personal and appointment data is retained only for the duration necessary to deliver clinical care, fulfill operational requirements, and comply with medical record retention regulations.

6. Cookies and Browsing Technologies

Our website may use standard cookies and lightweight analytics to support site navigation, remember your session preferences, and ensure responsive page loading across mobile and desktop devices. You can configure your web browser to disable or decline cookies at any time without impacting your ability to reach our clinic directly.

7. Your Privacy Rights

As a patient and visitor, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal contact details we hold on record for your appointments.
  • Request immediate correction or update of inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • Request that non-essential communications (such as appointment reminder notices) be paused or modified.

8. Policy Updates

Dhawan Skin and Laser Clinic reserves the right to periodically review and update this Privacy Policy to reflect advancements in technology, clinic practices, or legal obligations. Any revisions will be published on this page with the updated revision date.

9. Contact Our Clinic
